Kenny: Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Kenny stores your public Instagram username, the comments and direct messages you sent to it, its replies, and a small profile (message count, language preference, notable moments). You can ask for all of it to be deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Fill in the issue form, choose "Delete my data" and include your Instagram username. This creates a public GitHub issue, so add nothing private. Or send a direct message to @kennies.ai on Instagram saying "Delete my data" from the account you want deleted.
  2. We will confirm that the request came from that account, then delete your profile and the stored comments and replies linked to your username.
  3. Deletion is normally completed within 30 days.

Removing your comments yourself

You can delete your own comment on Instagram at any time. That does not remove a copy Kenny may have already stored, so use the steps above for a full deletion.
