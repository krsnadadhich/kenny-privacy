Kenny Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Kenny is an experimental AI chat character built by an individual developer. It replies to comments on the Instagram account @kennies.ai and may also chat in other places such as Discord or YouTube Live chat. This page explains what information Kenny handles and why.

What Kenny collects

When you comment on a post that Kenny is watching, Kenny receives:

  • the text of your comment,
  • your public Instagram username,
  • the time of the comment.

Kenny does not access your direct messages, followers, contacts, location, email address or any private account data.

What Kenny stores

  • Your username, your comment, and Kenny's reply, together with how Kenny decided to respond.
  • A small profile so Kenny can recognise returning people: how many messages you sent, your preferred language style, and a few notable moments (for example, "tried to break Kenny's rules").

This information is stored in a private database on the developer's own computer or server. It is not published and it is not sold.

How the information is used

  • To write a reply to your comment.
  • To remember returning people and keep Kenny's behaviour consistent.
  • To test and improve Kenny's safety rules (for example, how well it resists attempts to misuse it). Examples of comments may be kept as test cases; if any are ever published, usernames are removed.

Third-party services

To write replies, the text of your comment and your username are sent to Google's Gemini API. Kenny's replies are posted through the Instagram API provided by Meta. These services process data under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

Kenny does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, except with the services listed above that are needed to run it.

Retention

Information is kept for as long as Kenny is being tested or operated, or until you ask for it to be deleted.

Your choices

  • You can delete your own comment on Instagram at any time.
  • You can ask for everything Kenny stored about you to be deleted. See how to request data deletion.

Automated replies

Kenny is an AI. Its replies are generated automatically and are marked with a robot emoji. They may be wrong, silly or rude in a joking way, and should not be treated as advice.

Children

Kenny is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Kenny changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/krsnadadhich/kenny-privacy/issues, or message @kennies.ai on Instagram.
