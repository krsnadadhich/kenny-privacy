Kenny Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Kenny is an experimental AI chat character built by an individual developer. It lives on the Instagram account @kennies.ai and may also chat in other places such as Discord or YouTube Live chat. This page explains what Kenny can do, what information it handles and why.

What Kenny can do

  • Reply to comments on the Instagram posts it watches. Replies are in character, often in Hinglish when you write in Hinglish, and start with a robot emoji so you know it is an AI.
  • Reply to direct messages from anyone who messages @kennies.ai. Several quick messages in a row are answered together, and replies go out as short texts, only within Instagram's 24-hour messaging window. Your DM is read by an AI, not a human, and the developer may review conversations to fix problems.
  • Look at images that anyone sends in a DM, so it can react to them. Please do not send images of other people, private documents or anything sensitive. GIFs from Instagram's GIF picker are not visible to Kenny.
  • Recognise returning people and remember small things about them, such as how often they show up and notable moments, so it can talk about them (and about people you ask it about) from what it actually knows.
  • Have its own mind. A few times a day Kenny may write a private note to itself, or post a new comment on one of its own posts (at most one a day).
  • Use moods and tones. Kenny can be sarcastic, savage or gentle depending on the message. It does roast, but follows hard limits (no family, looks, health, identity or slurs) and is kind to anyone who seems to be struggling.
  • Say no. Kenny takes no orders. It refuses prompt-injection attempts, requests for private information, harassment and links.
  • Also available on Discord and YouTube Live chat for private tests and streams.

What Kenny cannot do

  • It cannot browse the web, search, or watch videos.
  • It cannot delete, report, restrict or follow anyone, or take any action on your account.
  • It does not read your followers, contacts, location, email address or any private account data.
  • It cannot see your DMs unless you message @kennies.ai yourself.

What Kenny collects

When you comment on a post Kenny is watching, or message @kennies.ai, Kenny receives:

  • the text of your comment or message (and any image you send),
  • your public Instagram username,
  • the time it was sent.

What Kenny stores

  • Your username, your comments and DMs, and Kenny's replies, together with how Kenny decided to respond.
  • A small profile so Kenny can recognise returning people: days seen, language style, a relationship label, a few short snippets of what you said, and notable moments (for example, "tried to break Kenny's rules").
  • Images are analysed to produce a short description for that reply. The image file itself is not kept.
  • Kenny's own private notes and posts.

This information is stored in a private database on the developer's own computer. It is not published and it is not sold.

How the information is used

  • To write a reply to your comment or DM.
  • To remember returning people and keep Kenny's behaviour consistent.
  • To test and improve Kenny's safety rules. Examples of messages may be kept as test cases; if any are ever published, usernames are removed.

Third-party services

To write replies, the text of your comment or message, your username and any images you send are sent to AI providers: Google's Gemini API, and Groq as a backup when Gemini is unavailable. A small model running on the developer's own computer may also be used. Kenny's replies are posted through the Instagram API provided by Meta, and a tunnel service (ngrok) may relay Instagram's notifications to the developer's computer. These services process data under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

Kenny does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, except with the services listed above that are needed to run it.

Retention

Information is kept for as long as Kenny is being tested or operated, or until you ask for it to be deleted.

Your choices

  • You can delete your own comment on Instagram at any time.
  • You can simply not message or comment. Kenny only sees what you send to it.
  • You can ask for everything Kenny stored about you to be deleted. See how to request data deletion, or report an issue.

Automated replies

Kenny is an AI. Its replies are generated automatically and public replies are marked with a robot emoji. They may be wrong, silly or rude in a joking way, and should not be treated as advice.

Children

Kenny is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Kenny changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions or requests: use the issue form (it creates a public GitHub issue), or message @kennies.ai on Instagram.
